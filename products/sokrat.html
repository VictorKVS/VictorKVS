Showcase / research prototype

Sokrat

An evidence-gated research prototype exploring bounded iterative research, orchestration, knowledge context, evaluation and final synthesis. It is not presented as a verified MVP or production system.

Problem

One-pass generation is often insufficient for complex research and engineering questions. Sokrat explores a repeatable session model where context and constraints are passed through an orchestrated research loop before a final synthesis is produced.

Verified repository surface

The repository contains research_engine/, a prototype-local knowledge_base/, sokrat_core/, separated unit/integration test directories, test_research.py and demo_integration.py.

A historical commit dated 2026-02-26 records a 13/13 test integration checkpoint. This is preserved as history, not treated as a fresh clean-host proof.

Flow

Task + constraints → SessionContext → ResearchOrchestrator → knowledge context / rounds → evaluation → final synthesis → session history

Current limitations

No current clean-host reproduction report has been recorded in the portfolio audit. Production security, scalability and reliability are not claimed. The repository-local knowledge store is historical prototype infrastructure and is not the canonical portfolio knowledge layer.

Promotion gate

Fresh environment install, current unit/integration test execution, reproducible demo, dependency/configuration hygiene review, negative cases, and an explicit integration boundary with KNOWLEDGE_CORE.

Ecosystem role

Sokrat is retained as an independent research lineage branch. It is conceptually related to later iterative research, critic and knowledge-driven engineering ideas, but is not merged into or presented as implementation evidence for PX00.
