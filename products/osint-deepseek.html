OSINT DeepSeek

Evidence-preserving OSINT worker for structured research tasks, provenance capture, bounded follow-up research and reproducible analyst handoff. It is evolving as an OSINT supplier for the FATHER / knowledge-factory ecosystem.

Problem

OSINT workflows often mix collection, interpretation and final claims, making it difficult to reproduce where evidence came from, what failed and why a conclusion changed. This project separates collection from analyst judgment and preserves provenance as an explicit engineering contract.

What is verified today

  • Frozen DEV v1 baseline with a clean-checkout verification path.
  • Python 3.12 DEV environment.
  • 21 automated tests PASS in the frozen baseline.
  • scripts/run_dev_osint.py and scripts/run_dev_pipeline.py are verified DEV runners.
  • Independent source observations are preserved even when payloads match.
  • Payload reuse is SHA-256 based without losing source provenance.
  • File-only material is hashed from original bytes and missing files fail explicitly.
  • Follow-up research accumulates evidence across cycles while loops remain hard-bounded.
  • Collector failures remain isolated and visible.
  • Telegram collection is abstracted from a specific transport implementation.

Engineering contract

Research Task → OSINT Collection → Material Package + Provenance → Analyst → Socrates / Verification → accepted DEV output

The worker does not decide what is true and does not autonomously publish knowledge. Analyst and verification layers remain separate.

Current milestone

M5 — Telegram Radar. Requirements, reuse/commercial review, security/supply-chain review, donor verification and a TDLib proof-of-concept precede any production transport decision. GramJS comparison is conditional on a material evidence gap.

What is not yet claimed

  • No production readiness.
  • No proven real MTProto session operations.
  • No proven Tor/proxy rotation production gateway.
  • No production secrets infrastructure or production LLM routing.
  • No generic Artifact ingestion, local transcription or autonomous Knowledge Gate publication yet.
  • Production RBAC, privileged access, patch/vulnerability management, backup/restore, incident response and operational monitoring remain future requirements.
